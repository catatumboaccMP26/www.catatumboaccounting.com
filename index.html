FAQ

Questions We Get
All the Time

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is the difference between bookkeeping and accounting?
Bookkeeping is the daily recording and organizing of financial transactions — it's the foundation. Accounting takes those records and turns them into financial statements, tax returns, and strategic insights. We offer both, and most clients need both.
Do I need a CPA? Can you handle my taxes?
Most small businesses don't need a CPA. We are PTIN registered, which means we can legally prepare and file your business tax returns. A CPA is only required for audited financial statements, which most small businesses never need.
What is cash vs accrual accounting?
Cash basis records money when it moves — simple for most small businesses. Accrual basis records revenue when earned and expenses when incurred — required for larger companies or investors. We offer both.
What does a Fractional CFO actually do?
A Fractional CFO gives you executive-level financial strategy without a full-time salary. Cash flow planning, budgets, investor preparation — for a fraction of the cost. Ideal for businesses doing $500K–$5M in revenue.
Can you form my LLC in any state?
Yes — we handle LLC, S-Corp, and C-Corp formations in all 50 US states. Service includes state filing, EIN application, Operating Agreement, and registered agent setup.
How does payroll pricing work?
Flat rate $75–$150/mo for up to 5 employees. For larger teams, $12–$20 per employee per month. Both include tax filings, direct deposit, and year-end W-2 preparation.
Do you serve Spanish-speaking clients?
Absolutely — fully bilingual service is one of our core strengths. We serve clients completely in Spanish, from initial consultation through monthly reports and tax filings.
How does the Client Portal work?
Every client gets a private portal to view monthly reports, download financials, upload documents directly to your accountant, sign documents electronically, track tax deadlines, and pay invoices. Available 24/7, included in all plans.
