FAQ

Questions We Get
All the Time

Everything you need to know before getting started. Don't see your question? Book a free call and ask us directly.

What is the difference between bookkeeping and accounting?
Bookkeeping is the daily recording and organizing of financial transactions — it's the foundation. Accounting takes those records and turns them into financial statements, tax returns, and strategic insights. We offer both, and most clients need both.
Do I need a CPA? Can you handle my taxes?
Most small businesses don't need a CPA — they need accurate books, clean financial statements, and a reliable tax preparer. We are PTIN registered, which means we can legally prepare and file your business tax returns. A CPA is only required for audited financial statements, which most small businesses never need.
What is cash vs accrual accounting? Which do I need?
Cash basis records money when it moves — simple and easy for most small businesses. Accrual basis records revenue when earned and expenses when incurred, regardless of when money moves — required for larger companies and those seeking investors or loans. We offer both. Most of our clients start with cash and move to accrual as they grow.
What does a Fractional CFO actually do?
A Fractional CFO gives you executive-level financial strategy without a full-time salary. We help you understand your numbers, plan cash flow, build budgets, prepare for investors or lenders, and make smarter financial decisions — for a fraction of the cost. Ideal for growing businesses doing $500K–$5M in revenue.
Can you help me form my LLC or corporation in any state?
Yes — we handle LLC, S-Corp, and C-Corp formations in all 50 US states. Our service includes the state filing, EIN application with the IRS, Operating Agreement or Bylaws, and registered agent setup. We'll also advise you on which structure makes the most sense for your specific situation and tax goals.
How does payroll pricing work?
We offer two models. For small teams (up to 5 employees), a flat monthly rate of $75–$150 is typically most cost-effective. For larger teams, we charge $12–$20 per employee per month — so the cost scales with your team size. Both options include tax filings, direct deposit, and W-2 preparation.
Do you serve Spanish-speaking clients?
Absolutely — fully bilingual service is one of our core strengths. We serve clients completely in Spanish, from initial consultation through monthly reports and tax filings. Our team is Venezuelan-founded and deeply connected to the Hispanic business community in the US.
How does the Client Portal work?
Every client gets access to a private portal where they can view monthly reports, download financial statements and tax documents, upload receipts or files to us, and send direct messages to their accountant. It's available 24/7 and included in all plans. Think of it as your personal financial dashboard.
